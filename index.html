Formula 01 · Optical Restoration

Gray-World Chromatic Normalization

backend/app.py · preprocess_underwater()
\[\mu_c = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum_{(x,y) \in \Omega} I_c(x,y), \qquad \mu_{\text{gray}} = \frac{\mu_R + \mu_G + \mu_B}{3}\] \[I'_c(x,y) = \operatorname{clip}\!\left( I_c(x,y) \cdot \frac{\mu_{\text{gray}}}{\mu_c},\; 0,\; 255 \right)\]
\mu_c = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum_{(x,y) \in \Omega} I_c(x,y), \quad \mu_{\text{gray}} = \frac{\mu_R + \mu_G + \mu_B}{3} \\ I'_c(x,y) = \operatorname{clip}\left( I_c(x,y) \cdot \frac{\mu_{\text{gray}}}{\mu_c},\; 0,\; 255 \right)

Why it's used: Eliminates dominant yellowish-brown and murky green color casts common in Indian coastal waters. By assuming the spatial average across the entire underwater scene is neutral achromatic gray, it computes per-channel gain scalars that restore lost red/blue wavelength energy.

Symbol Meaning
I_c(x,y) Input color channel (\(c \in \{B,G,R\}\))
\mu_c Spatial mean of channel \(c\)
\mu_{\text{gray}} Global equal-energy achromatic baseline
I'_c Chromatically balanced output pixel

\[I_{\text{sharp}} = 1.35 \cdot I_{\text{restored}} \;-\; 0.35 \cdot \bigl(I_{\text{restored}} * G_{\sigma=2.5}\bigr)\]

Why it's used: Marine debris submerged for extended periods develops algae growth and bio-fouling that softens object boundaries into the seabed. Unsharp masking subtracts a Gaussian low-pass blur (\(\sigma=2.5\)) to emphasize high-frequency edges of degraded plastic and derelict gear.

Symbol Meaning
G_\sigma Gaussian smoothing kernel (\(\sigma = 2.5\))
1.35 Identity scaling coefficient
−0.35 High-pass edge amplification weight

Formula 05 · SAHI & Detection

Intersection-over-Union (IoU) & Multi-Scale NMS

backend/app.py · compute_iou() & NMSBoxes()
\[\operatorname{IoU}(B_1, B_2) = \frac{\operatorname{Area}(B_1 \cap B_2)}{\operatorname{Area}(B_1 \cup B_2)}\] \[\mathcal{D}_{\text{final}} = \operatorname{NMS}\!\left(\{\mathbf{b}_k, c_k, s_k\},\; \tau_{\text{conf}} = 0.03,\; \tau_{\text{IoU}} = 0.40\right)\]
\operatorname{IoU}(B_1, B_2) = \frac{\operatorname{Area}(B_1 \cap B_2)}{\operatorname{Area}(B_1 \cup B_2)} \\ \mathcal{D}_{\text{final}} = \operatorname{NMS}\left(\{ \mathbf{b}_k, c_k, s_k \},\, \tau_{\text{conf}} = 0.03,\, \tau_{\text{IoU}} = 0.40 \right)

Why it's used: Multi-scale slicing and ensemble models generate overlapping candidate proposals for the same debris item. Global NMS suppresses redundant boxes when their pairwise IoU exceeds \(\tau_{\text{IoU}} = 0.40\), retaining the highest-confidence bounding box.

Symbol Meaning
B_1, B_2 Candidate bounding boxes being compared
\tau_{\text{conf}} Minimum confidence filter (0.03)
\tau_{\text{IoU}} NMS suppression threshold (0.40)
\mathcal{D}_{\text{final}} Deduplicated output detections
Eq. (5)

def compute_iou(box1, box2):
    x1, y1 = max(box1[0], box2[0]), max(box1[1], box2[1])
    x2, y2 = min(box1[2], box2[2]), min(box1[3], box2[3])
    inter = max(0.0, x2 - x1) * max(0.0, y2 - y1)
    area1 = max(0.0, box1[2] - box1[0]) * max(0.0, box1[3] - box1[1])
    area2 = max(0.0, box2[2] - box2[0]) * max(0.0, box2[3] - box2[1])
    union = area1 + area2 - inter
    return inter / union if union > 0 else 0.0

Formula 07 · Benthic Hazard

Seabed Coverage Ratio (SCR)

inferenceService.js
\[\operatorname{SCR} = \min\!\left(100,\; \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{M} \operatorname{Area}(B_i)}{W_{\text{frame}} \times H_{\text{frame}}} \times 100\right)\]
\operatorname{SCR} = \min\left(100, \, \frac{\sum_{i=1}^M \operatorname{Area}(B_i)}{W_{\text{frame}} \times H_{\text{frame}}} \times 100\right)

Why it's used: Item count alone fails to distinguish between 5 tiny bottle caps versus 5 massive derelict fishing nets or discarded vehicle tires. SCR calculates the true percentage of benthic substrate smothered by anthropogenic debris.

Formula 08 · Benthic Hazard

Continuous Ecological Hazard Index (EHI)

inferenceService.js
\[\operatorname{EHI} = \min\!\left(100,\;\max\!\left(0,\; 14\,N_{\text{plastic}} + 9\,N_{\text{metal}} + 6\,N_{\text{other}} + 3.5 \times \operatorname{SCR}\right)\right)\]
\operatorname{EHI} = \min\left(100,\, \max\left(0,\, 14 N_{\text{plastic}} + 9 N_{\text{metal}} + 6 N_{\text{other}} + 3.5 \times \operatorname{SCR} \right)\right)

Why it's used: Provides environmental authorities and cleanup fleets with a standardized 0–100 threat score. Weights reflect empirical marine toxicity and persistence: macro-plastics receive the highest penalty (14) due to microplastic fragmentation and trophic ingestion risks, followed by metals (9) for galvanic leaching, and substrate coverage (3.5).

Symbol Meaning
N_{\text{plastic}} Detected plastic item count — weight 14
N_{\text{metal}} Detected metal item count — weight 9
N_{\text{other}} Other debris (tires, nets) — weight 6
3.5 × SCR Spatial benthic smothering penalty
